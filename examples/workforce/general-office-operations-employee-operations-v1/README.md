# General Office Operations Employee Operations Fixture

Role Contract version 1.2.0 adds bounded KPI, SLA reporting, periodic reporting,
SLA-breach reporting, and internal escalation obligations without expanding the
1.1.0 capability or authority ceiling. WORK-700 compiles and separately approves,
delegates, and instantiates this immutable version. Routing identifiers name only
protected internal inbox classes; they are not people or external endpoints.

The WORK-700 Completion runner binds this exact source to a new signed Role
approval, subset delegation, active Role Instance, signed Operations Profile,
and signed internal routing registry. It evaluates five Cases from the
WORK-500/600 evidence chain, publishes only local protected inbox objects, and
removes its temporary store and audit roots before completion.
