# Training Compliance Follow-up Case

This execution-free fixture binds `safety.training.compliance_followup` to the
AI Safety Operations Employee Role. Subjects are opaque references. Completion
requires trusted completion records, zero remaining non-compliance, explicit
expired exceptions, follow-up evidence, and escalation evidence for overdue
exceptions.

The fixture does not connect to a training system, discover work, schedule
tasks, or perform a safety operation.
