instantiated for WORK-900. Its `limited-autonomy` environment admits only the
`kernel-e2e-name-save` Application Pack, `uia.set_value`, `uia.invoke`,

Shadow Role or the 1.2.0 operations Role.

admission remain non-executable evidence. Every actual action still requires
current Role, delegation, ownership, lease, Work Grant, policy, one-shot
activation, trusted KRN execution, fresh re-observation, and independent
verification. Policy unknown, authority exceeded, unsafe verification, and
other mandatory reason codes terminate autonomous continuation in exactly one
protected human-exception handoff.

limited-autonomy Profile, deployment approval, control commands, and bounded
human responses. It does not reuse WORK-800 signatures or turn Shadow
readiness into authority.
