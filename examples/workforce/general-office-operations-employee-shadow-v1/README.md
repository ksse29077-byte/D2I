# General Office Operations Employee Shadow Fixture

Role Contract version 1.3.0 is a separately compiled, approved, delegated, and
instantiated WORK-800 fixture. Its `shadow-mode` environment declares the
bounded `uia.set_value` classification as confirmation-only so the Shadow
Profile can evaluate that proposal without expanding the Role. WORK-800 never
issues that confirmation or any activation, and the Role cannot be substituted
for the 1.2.0 operations Role. It also adds the internal
`office-shadow-evaluation` reporting obligation bound to `shadow-cohort-close`,
`shadow-evaluation-report`, and the existing protected `office-supervision`
route.

The Shadow Profile may classify Application Pack operations for comparison,
but neither the Profile nor its capability classifications grant Role,
policy, confirmation, activation, KRN, adapter, lease, ownership, or Case
mutation authority. The reference operator acts under a separate signed
participation and assignment contract. AI proposals remain hidden until that
reference step and the fresh post-action observation are durable.

The Completion runner signs new Role approval, delegation, Role Instance,
Profile, readiness policy, participation, assignment, and cohort evidence. It
does not reuse 1.2.0 signatures.
